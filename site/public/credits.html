Credits

Plumb Search is built from open data. Each node keeps titles, short descriptions and counts, not whole pages. Thanks to everyone who makes these sources.

Wikipedia
Article titles and short descriptions, from Wikimedia's dumps. Text under CC BY-SA 4.0; page views are CC0.
Wikidata
Official websites, names, countries, kinds of site and official profiles. CC0.
Stack Overflow
Question titles, tags and views, from Stack Exchange's data dump. CC BY-SA 4.0.
ecosyste.ms
Package names, versions, licences and links for eight registries. CC BY-SA 4.0.
OpenStreetMap
Places. © OpenStreetMap contributors, under the Open Database License.
OpenAlex
The most cited papers. CC0.
Open Library
The most read books, from its dumps. CC0.
Podcast Index
Titles, authors, categories and popularity of the most popular podcasts. Free for any use.
MusicBrainz
Titles, artists, years and links (lyrics on Genius, Spotify, Apple Music, music videos) of the songs and albums most listened to, from its core data dump. CC0.
ListenBrainz
How many people listened to each song and album, from its API. CC0.
GitHub
Names, descriptions and stars of well-starred public repositories, from GitHub's API.
Tranco
The top million sites list, used for the first ranks.
Common Crawl
Web graph domain ranks, and optionally homepage titles and link text from its WAT files, under Common Crawl's terms of use.
The Block List Project
The adult sites list behind safe search. Public domain (Unlicense).
European Central Bank
Daily euro reference rates, for currency conversions.
BAAI/bge-small-en-v1.5
The embedding model behind search by meaning. MIT license.

Site titles and descriptions come from the sites' own homepages. The full list, with what each source is used for, is in the README.
